## 📊 Market Analysis & Competitive Edge

### The Problem
*   **Connectivity Barrier:** Existing apps like *Myschool* and *Pass.ng* often require a constant connection or have broken offline modes.
*   **Dated UX:** Cluttered interfaces with ads distract students during high-pressure study sessions.
*   **Generic Explanations:** Many platforms provide answers without the "why," especially for complex Science and Math problems.

### The Examply Solution
*   **True Offline-First:** Our "Library" system allows users to download entire yearly packs (JAMB 2024 Math, etc.) once and use them forever without a single byte of data.
*   **Science-First Pedagogy:** Optimized for Math, Physics, and Chemistry with full LaTeX support (KaTeX) so equations look like they do in the textbook.
*   **Focus Mode:** A minimal, distraction-free UI designed to induce "flow state" during practice.
*   **The SLM Advantage:** Unlike competitors who rely on expensive API calls, our future roadmap includes a Small Language Model that runs locally on the device, providing AI tutoring in "the woods" or the "big city" without internet.

## ✨ UI/UX Highlights

*   **Focus Mode:** A dedicated 'Study' vs 'Test' environment. Study mode provides instant, AI-curated explanations, while Test mode simulates the high-pressure exam environment.
*   **The Library:** A centralized hub for managing downloaded content. Features progress tracking, high scores, and easy deletion to save device storage.
*   **Typewriter Hero:** An engaging, motivational home screen that cycles through pedagogical quotes to keep students inspired.
*   **Professional Palette:** Uses 'Slate & Indigo'—a high-contrast, professional color scheme that reduces eye strain during long study sessions.

## 🔮 Future Vision: The Offline Tutor (SLM)

The ultimate goal of Examply is to eliminate the "Digital Divide." We are developing a specialized **Small Language Model (SLM)** designed to run locally on mid-range Android and iOS devices.

This means a student in a village with zero bars of signal can ask: *"How do I solve this quadratic equation?"* and get a step-by-step tutorial from an AI that lives entirely on their phone. No data, no latency, just learning.
